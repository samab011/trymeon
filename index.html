[ 05 ] Questions

The useful answers.

What exactly can an AI agent handle?

The questions your team answers twenty times a day — prices, availability, timings, location, order and application status — plus taking bookings straight into your calendar. We agree the scope in week one and set hard rules for what it must never answer on its own; anything outside that goes to a human with the full conversation attached.

Can it actually reply in Urdu?

Yes — Urdu script, Roman Urdu and English, answering in whichever one the customer used. Most Pakistani customers type Roman Urdu, so that is what we tune for hardest.

What do we need for a virtual property tour?

Access to the property and roughly two hours per unit. We capture it, build the tour, and embed it in your listing with enquiry capture built in, so a buyer who has just walked the property can ask a question without leaving the page. Show homes and unit types work well too — you capture once and sell many.

How do you know the website is actually converting?

We agree what counts as a win before we build — an enquiry, a booking, a call — and instrument it properly. You get a monthly read on what moved and what we would change next, in plain language rather than a dashboard nobody opens.

How do we pay you?

A PKR invoice, monthly, by bank transfer or cheque. Sales tax is added where applicable, and withholding is handled on the invoice for both filer and non-filer status. No dollar billing and no foreign card required.

Our customers are on slow connections. Will this be heavy?

No. Page weight is budgeted before we build, and tours stream progressively rather than loading all at once — so they open on 4G outside the big cities too.

Do you replace our developers?

No. We plan, design and produce — and we build front-end when you want us to. If you have an in-house team, they get a spec and a component library they'll actually enjoy using.

Who owns the output?

You do. Files, source, tours, tokens and agent prompts are handed over at the end of every engagement, no exit fee.
