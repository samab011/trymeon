[ 05 ] Questions

The useful answers.

Can the assistant actually reply in Urdu?

Yes — Urdu script, Roman Urdu and English, and it answers in whichever one the customer used. Most Pakistani customers type Roman Urdu, so that's what we tune for hardest. Anything it isn't confident about goes to your team instead of being guessed at.

We sell on Daraz and Instagram. Do we even need our own site?

Sometimes no — and we'll tell you that in week one rather than sell you a build. But marketplace fees and zero customer data catch up with most brands. If your own store makes sense, we plan it so the marketplace keeps running alongside it, not against it.

How do we pay you?

A PKR invoice, monthly, by bank transfer or cheque. Sales tax is added where applicable, and withholding is handled on the invoice for both filer and non-filer status. No dollar billing and no foreign card required.

Our customers are on slow connections. Will this be heavy?

No. Media is budgeted per page before we shoot a frame, loops are compressed and lazy-loaded, and everything has a reduced-motion fallback. A typical page stays under 400kb on mobile so it opens outside the big cities too.

Do you replace our developers?

No. We plan, design and produce — and we build front-end when you want us to. If you have an in-house team, they get a spec and a component library they'll actually enjoy using.

Who owns the output?

You do. Files, source, tokens and prompts are handed over at the end of every engagement, no exit fee.
